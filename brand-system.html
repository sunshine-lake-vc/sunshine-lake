05 · Motion

Slow, warm, never busy.

Transitions — 0.3–0.6s on cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1). Fades + small translate (8–28px); scale ≤ 1.05.
Ambient loops — slow, 6–14s, ease-in-out alternate: star pulse / nova, water caustics on photo tiles, twinkling stars, sequential count-ups on stats.
Carousels — one page fades to the next; sun-yellow progress bar + dots; auto-advance ~6–7s, pause on hover.
Always respect prefers-reduced-motion — drop loops and turbulence.
06 · Voice & guardrails

Editorial, plain-spoken, a little cosmic.

Short declarative serif headlines. Concrete nouns over adjectives. Confidence without hype. Recurring motifs: forged by extreme origins, the spark / the star, out with the old, the aperture, the lake.

Do

  • Lead with a short serif line, support with one sans paragraph.
  • Use sun-yellow as a scalpel — one accent per view.
  • Let whitespace and the deep navy breathe.
  • Name real people/companies; let quotes be the proof.

Don't (public site)

  • No performance figures / multiples (no "19×", DPI, fund returns).
  • No "global founders" thesis phrasing; no pre-fund seed track record.
  • No gradients-on-gradients or more than one accent color per section.
  • No stock-photo gloss — the imagery is candid and duotoned.
