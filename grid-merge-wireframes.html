Testimonials × the photo grid

Three ways to merge the "In their words" quotes into the snapshot wall so the strong quotes stay big and always visible (no hover to read them). Grey = photo tile, gold-outlined = quote tile.

1Stacked & merged — quotes on top, photo wall beneath
One section, one header. A row of large quote cards sits directly on top of the photo wall (shared background, no gap). Quotes are the hero; the photos become the texture underneath. Closest to what's live now, lowest risk.

Best if: most quote-givers don't have photos. Quotes and photos stay cleanly separated but read as one block. Carousel still works for the quote row.
2Quote tiles in the wall — double-wide, photo + quote
A single mosaic. Photo tiles stay 1×1; any person who has a quote gets a double-wide tile — their photo on one side, their quote always visible on the other. Quotes punctuate the photo wall in place.
Best if: the quote-giver is the person in the photo. Most integrated look, but needs a photo for each quoted person and careful placement so the grid stays tidy.
3Photo-backed quote cards — big 2×2, magazine style
The quote sits over a darkened version of the person's photo as a large 2×2 block, interspersed among the small photo tiles like pull-quotes in a photo spread. Most editorial and dramatic; quote always legible on the image.
Best if: we have strong, well-cropped photos of the quoted founders. Highest visual impact; the quote and the face are one unit. Most design/photo work.
